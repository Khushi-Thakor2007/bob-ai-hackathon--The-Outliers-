# Problem Statement: Supply Chain Disruption & Fleet Inefficiencies

## Background
Global freight logistics operators manage hundreds of multimodal consignments moving simultaneously across ports, expressways, rail yards, and air cargo terminals. These networks operate under tight delivery deadlines and strict temperature parameters for biologicals and pharmaceuticals.

## The Problem
When disruptions occur—such as the JNPT union port strike, Arabian Sea storms, or highway closures—freight dispatchers face critical visibility blind spots:
1. Identifying which specific shipments are exposed requires manual spreadsheet cross-referencing that takes hours.
2. Estimating delays and risk levels is subjective, leading to missed mitigation windows.
3. Cold-chain IoT temperature excursions go undetected until delivery, resulting in catastrophic pharmaceutical spoilage.
4. Nearby carrier fleet assets sit idle with less than 25% utilization while disrupted freight lanes suffer acute capacity shortages.

## Who is Affected
- Multimodal Logistics Operations Managers
- Supply Chain Control Tower Directors
- Freight Dispatch Coordinators
- Cold-Chain Pharmaceutical Transport Officers

## Cost of the Problem
Millions of dollars in exposed cargo value, penalty fees for SLA breaches, pharmaceutical shipment spoilage, and wasted fleet overhead.
